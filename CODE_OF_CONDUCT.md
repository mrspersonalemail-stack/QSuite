# Code of Conduct

QSuite adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies to project discussions, issues, pull requests and people representing
this community. The linked standard is the governing text; the following is a
short practical summary.

Treat participants with dignity. Welcome different backgrounds and experience,
listen to feedback, and focus criticism on the work. Harassment, threats,
discriminatory abuse, unwanted sexual attention and disclosure of another person's
private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers should explain moderation decisions, protect confidential reports and
apply proportionate responses, from a correction or warning to temporary or
permanent exclusion for serious or repeated misconduct. People affected by an
incident should not be required to debate it publicly. Maintainers involved in a
complaint should recuse themselves from handling it.

Report conduct concerns confidentially to
[qsuite-reports@proton.me](mailto:qsuite-reports@proton.me). Include the relevant
context and links where safe. Do not post a reporter's identity or sensitive
evidence in a public issue. Maintainers should limit access to reports to those
handling the concern and recuse themselves when personally involved.

Attribution: Contributor Covenant 2.1, maintained by the Contributor Covenant
community. The reporting inbox is dedicated to QSuite.
